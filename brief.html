01

Maritime / Hormuz

Severe → Confidence: High
  • Iran state media confirms Iraqi oil tankers granted Hormuz passage following Ghalibaf Baghdad visit (Reuters/IRNA 22 Aug Hard T1); volume and terms unspecified — framed explicitly as diplomatic concession to Baghdad, not general reopening.
  • UKMTO/CNN analysis (22 Aug): ~20% of pre-war average transits; US Navy fired on Panama-flagged cargo vessel attempting to reach Iranian ports; northern (Iran-controlled) route dominant; PGSA permit system operational.
  • US military reports escorting 660 million barrels of crude through Hormuz since early May (TradingEconomics/Reuters); represents authorized corridor only — does not indicate normalization.

Why it matters: The Iraqi tanker concession is Iran's most significant PGSA gatekeeping signal since the July collapse — it proves selective access is a deliberate leverage tool, not technical limitation. Baghdad's monthly oil revenue has collapsed from $7–8 bn to $1.2–1.9 bn; Iran is using this pain to maintain political alignment with Iraq while keeping the Hormuz lever cocked for the US-facing negotiation.

Implication: No change to Cape unconditional routing. PGSA concession to Iraq does not create a precedent for third-party commercial transits without permit. War-risk insurance premiums remain elevated. P(Hormuz normalization within 14 days) = 0.08.

Sources: Reuters (Hard T1) 22 Aug; IRNA (Hard T1) 22 Aug; CNN/UKMTO (Hard T1) 22 Aug; TradingEconomics (T2) 22 Aug; list25/CBS (T2) 22 Aug corroborating Reuters.

This dynamic limits Iran's ability to fully close Hormuz to all buyers without fracturing its regional coalition.

Implication: $90 floor structural. P(Brent above $95 within 7 days) = 0.50 — Economic D-Day catalyst Mon 25 Aug plus parliament risk window; ±5pp band [0.45–0.55]. P(Brent above $100 within 7 days) = 0.22 (↑ from 0.18).

  • Saudi Petroline / Yanbu: 14th consecutive operational check — no satellite-confirmed impairment. Iraq–Turkey Ceyhan signed as partial alternative for ~0.5 Mbbl/day increment (Al-Monitor 22 Aug T1).

The flag-immunity concept applies only to Houthi kinetic; piracy makes no such distinction.

Implication: Cape unconditional. BAM requires separate clearance of all three vectors before any transit consideration. Saudi Petroline remains the only fully operational high-volume alt route. Iraq–Turkey Ceyhan provides marginal buffer but insufficient for regional rebalancing.

Methodology

This brief uses a tiered signal-weighting framework. Only verified physical events from primary sources can move the threat assessment; trend movement requires confirmed change versus the prior 72h. The system continuously calibrates against outcomes — when predictions miss, the underlying heuristics are sharpened. The full operating ruleset is kept private to preserve analytical edge against adversarial reading.

Sources

Inputs span six tiers, weighted by track record. Specific outlets and platforms are not disclosed publicly.

  • Tier 1 — Primary & official. Maritime authorities, defense commands, foreign ministries, treasury sanctions desks. First-party communiqués that count as Hard signal.
  • Tier 2 — Specialized commercial intelligence. Vessel-tracking platforms (AIS-based), aircraft-tracking platforms (ADS-B), tanker-flow analytics, energy-cargo intelligence, piracy-reporting bureaus. Operational data with measurable track records.
  • Tier 3 — Analytical institutions. Geopolitical and security think tanks with rigorous publication standards and demonstrable calibration history on this region.
  • Tier 4 — Commercial analysts & columnists. Independent practitioners and commodity-desk analysts whose published positions can be back-tested over multi-year horizons.
  • Tier 5 — Wire services & reputable regional press. Western wire services, top-tier business and political dailies, regional papers across the Gulf, Israel, and Iran (read with editorial-line caveats), and analytical defense-and-policy publications.
  • Tier 6 — Excluded by default. Anonymous OSINT accounts, op-eds, social media, "sources say" without attribution, AI-generated summaries, forum content. Enter the brief only after independent corroboration by Tier 1–3.
