## Executive summary

Iran presented conditions for Hormuz reopening (blockade lift, frozen assets, resistance-front ceasefire); Witkoff said he "feels very good." Both developments drove Scenario A's largest single-day revision (+14pp to 28%). Key uncertainties: Rubio has not yet confirmed or denied the MoU framework; SNSC chief Rezaei's concurrent hardline statement creates an Araghchi–SNSC split signal; no follow-on meeting date confirmed.
