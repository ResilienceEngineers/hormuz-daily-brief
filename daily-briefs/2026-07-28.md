## Executive Summary

The Trump–Netanyahu White House summit on 28 July produced no de-escalation framework text and no joint public statement on Iran beyond reaffirmation of the nuclear red line. Iran explicitly denied direct US negotiations ("We currently have no negotiations with the United States") while framing Oman-mediated Hormuz talks as sovereign stability discussions, not concessions. Brent settled ~$84/bbl on 28 Jul — a 16% three-session decline driven by diplomatic optimism — but structural supply disruption remained unchanged: Hormuz at ~6 vessels/day, Bab al-Mandeb recovering to 28 vessels/day (still 89% below pre-crisis baseline), Jazan Aramco refinery on fire, and Yanbu under kinetic threat. The four-day operational pause appeared to hold through the summit — until the IRGC launched a surprise ballistic missile strike on US forces in Jordan at 5:45 pm ET, which CENTCOM intercepted and responded to with joint US–Saudi precision strikes against IRGC-directed militia sites in eastern Iraq.
