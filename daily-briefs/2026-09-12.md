## Executive Summary

Day 197 marks the first day of the Saudi triple-pathway blockade: Hormuz effectively closed (~6–13 transits/day vs 85 baseline), the East-West pipeline shut since 11 September after Iraqi-drone strikes on pumping stations in the Riyadh and Medina regions, and Bab al-Mandeb now under Houthi direct-fire artillery range from Mayyun (Perim) Island, seized 11 Sep. The Houthi Supreme Political Council announced a ceasefire of hostilities on Yemen's western Red Sea coast — a territorial consolidation pause, not a commercial shipping-lane opening. Structural Brent band revised to $100–115 absent Salalah breakthrough; Goldman $120 tail active if pipeline remains shut past 7 days.
