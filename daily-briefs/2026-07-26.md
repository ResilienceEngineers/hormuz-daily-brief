## Executive Summary

A mutual 48-hour operational pause between the US and Iran — the longest since fighting resumed in July — opened the highest-stakes diplomatic window since the June MoU, with Netanyahu travelling to Washington for a Trump summit on 29 July focused on Iran strategy. However, the Houthi front escalated independently: Jizan Aramco refinery was struck and ignited on 25 July, Saudi defenses intercepted two ballistic missiles targeting Yanbu on 26 July, and Bab al-Mandeb commercial transits fell to 11 vessels — lowest in months. Brent dropped sharply to $90.95 on the diplomatic signal, breaking the $95 structural floor. The core bifurcation sharpens: US–Iran axis showing most credible de-escalation signal in 5 weeks, while Houthi proxy axis independently escalates Saudi energy infrastructure.
