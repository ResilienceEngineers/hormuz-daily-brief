## Executive Summary

The Salalah ministerial between GCC foreign ministers and Iran's FM — the first such meeting since the February outbreak of war — was confirmed for 14 September in Oman's southern coastal city, with Iran briefing that Muscat and Tehran had already agreed the details of entry and exit routes through the Strait of Hormuz. An Iranian official pre-signalled no deal would be signed; Bahrain announced it would not attend pending restoration of diplomatic ties. Simultaneously, Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline remained offline at day 2 following Iraqi-origin drone strikes on pumping stations in the Riyadh and Medina regions on 10–11 September, eliminating the kingdom's last functioning crude export pathway. *Houthi forces completed their takeover of Yemen's entire Red Sea coastline including Mayyun (Perim) Island, establishing artillery-range capability over Bab al-Mandeb.* Brent settled at ~$104 following a 4% diplomatic-discount dip on the Salalah announcement, but the triple-pathway Saudi export blockade and the Houthi territorial consolidation drove the dual-chokepoint re-escalation scenario to 67% modal probability.
