Houthis claimed a major missile-and-drone strike on Saudi Aramco's Yanbu export terminal and Khamis Mushait airbase on 16 Sep, with satellite imagery showing intense fires at Yanbu on 15 Sep; Saudi government/Aramco have not confirmed damage. The East-West pipeline remains offline day 6, with Aramco pursuing a partial bypass targeting ~half capacity within days and full restoration in ~6 weeks (Bloomberg). No formal Bab al-Mandeb closure declaration despite full Houthi Red Sea coast control. Iran-Oman temporary Hormuz route agreed (Aug 26) but no new Hard signing confirmed. Threat 4/5 Severe holds; trend assessed → Same, Confidence Medium.
