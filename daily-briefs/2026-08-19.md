## Executive summary

The UAE suspended all trade, commercial exchange, and financial transactions with Iran on 19 August (AP, WaPo, The National — T1) following confirmation that two Iranian ballistic missiles were fired toward UAE territory on 18 August, one landing inside UAE territorial waters. This is the single most consequential economic decoupling event of the conflict, severing Iran's primary re-export hub and sanctions-bypass gateway. The IRGC "fully offensive" posture declaration (Reuters, 18 Aug) remains in force. The 60-day MOU negotiating window expired 17 August without talks beginning. Trump threatened to bomb Oman on 17 August; Oman subsequently reported "no new developments" in Iran talks (CNN, 18 Aug). Iran parliament Hormuz bill continues advancing toward a plenary floor vote — no date set as of 06:00 UTC 19 Aug. Brent closed at $91.86 on 19 Aug, the 5th consecutive rising session.
